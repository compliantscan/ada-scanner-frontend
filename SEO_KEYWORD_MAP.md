## Cannibalization guardrails

1. **Homepage vs WCAG checker:** The homepage owns the agency platform and commercial product category. The checker owns immediate scan-tool intent.
2. **Homepage vs pricing:** The homepage explains value and workflow. Pricing explains plans, costs, limits, and purchase choices.
3. **Audit checklist vs testing comparison:** The checklist owns the complete audit sequence. The comparison article owns the choice and combination of testing methods.
4. **Blog listing vs articles:** The listing is a resource hub. It should summarize and distribute authority, not target either article’s exact query.
5. **Contact vs commercial pages:** Contact should help branded visitors reach the company, not act as another product landing page.

Use the primary keyword naturally in the title, H1, opening copy, and one or two internal anchors where appropriate. Do not force exact-match anchors from every page; vary anchors while preserving descriptive meaning.
